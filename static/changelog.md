# What's new in CodeCoach

## 3.6.0

- **Practice that looks like your quiz.** For classes, your coach now takes topics from the whole unit and question styles from the practice quiz and past quizzes. Each skill gets asked at least two ways (one the practice quiz doesn't use), and it only goes deeper once you can do the quiz-style version.
- **Coverage map.** Your coach lists everything your next quiz can ask and tracks which skills are solid. Today shows it ("Quiz 6: 7/12 skills solid") with **Study the gaps**; it's also in Quick review, Progress and practice exams.
- **Tests that are right the first time.** For harder problems your coach also writes a few near-miss wrong answers, and the problem is only given to you if every one of them fails. Loose tests get fixed before you see the problem, not halfway through.
- **Whole Java classes.** Problems can ask for a full `public class X { ... }` like the real quiz, and tests check the exact signatures (public/private, static, return and parameter types), naming what's off.
- **Clearer errors.** If your code compiles but doesn't match the signature the problem asks for, you're told what to change (e.g. "`winner` should be `static`") instead of "test setup problem".
- **Add the materials for your next quiz.** When your next quiz has no lessons or practice quiz in Materials, Today asks for them.

## 3.5.0

- **Warm-ups that clear your reviews.** Every session starts with about 5 minutes on what's due: a few toolkit lines and one quick question per topic due for review, and those reviews now get recorded, so topics stop sitting at "due".
- **Lines that keep slipping get a new approach.** If you've missed the same one-line drill twice, your coach breaks it into parts, has you type variations of it and uses it in a small fill-in, instead of drilling it the same way again.
- **Words first.** Before the first code example on a new topic, your coach makes sure you can name the parts (field, constructor, parameter...). Review sheets now start with a **Terms** list.
- **Is your next quiz still right?** Today asks when the date in your course profile has passed (or there is none). Update it in a few seconds; your coach plans around it.
- **Unfinished problems** from earlier sessions show up on Today with a Finish it button.
- **Update notices.** When a new version is out you get a small notice with **Update now**, without pressing Check now. CodeCoach checks when it opens and every few hours.
- **What's new.** After an update, this list shows up once.

## 3.4.1

- One clock instead of two: the clock at the top shows your time on the problem (counting down when there's a time limit), and it's exactly what your coach is told.
- The clock pauses whenever CodeCoach isn't the window in front, and continues when you come back.

## 3.4.0

- **Update now:** updates install in a few seconds inside the app. No more downloading and replacing the app (except for rare big changes).
- **Undo this update** in Settings > App.

## 3.3.0

- **Quick review:** a read-only study sheet for any topic or past session, and a live Review panel beside the chat.

## 3.2.0

- **Practice exams** with lockdown, a real clock and a full report, then a debrief with your coach.
- **Outcomes:** learning gain, retention and transfer, measured from your own work.

## 3.1.0

- Cost estimates for every AI provider, sync conflict protection, faster sessions and a safer code runner.
