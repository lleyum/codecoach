Learn to code with a coach that remembers

CodeCoach is a study app for any programming language. An AI coach teaches you, quizzes you, drills syntax and gives you real problems with tests that run on your own computer. It keeps notes on what you've mastered and brings topics back right before you'd forget them.

A ladder, not a quiz bank

Every topic climbs from R0 syntax drills and R1 code tracing to R5 writing from scratch and R6 timed problems. A topic counts as mastered after three new problems solved without hints.

Reviews that come back on time

Solved topics return after 2, 5, 12, 30 and 75 days, mixed with other topics. Struggling pulls the next review closer.

Fits your class or your goal

Drop in your lecture notes, slides and practice quizzes, and the coach follows your class's format and AI policy. Or start a general track for C++, Python, Java, Go or Rust.

❯ your data

Your data stays yours.

All your notes, solutions and progress live in a plain folder on your computer. Read or edit any of it in any text editor, or open the folder as an Obsidian vault. Sync it with the tools you already use. There is no CodeCoach account and no CodeCoach cloud.

CodeCoach Library/
├─ Courses/
│  └─ Intro to Java/
│     ├─ _course.md          course profile + AI policy

│     ├─ Mistakes.md · Pattern Library.md
│     ├─ materials/          your class content

└─ CodeCoach/
   ├─ sessions/             resume any session
   ├─ snippets/
   └─ Learner Profile.md    how you learn best
  • Plain Markdown. TextEdit, Notepad, VS Code and Obsidian all open it. If CodeCoach disappeared tomorrow, your work would still be there.
  • Sync for free. Put the folder in iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive, or track it with Git or Syncthing. Point CodeCoach on your other computer at the same folder.
  • Keys stay local. AI keys live in ~/.codecoach/config.json on each computer. They never go into your Library or an export.
  • No tracking. CodeCoach talks only to the AI you choose.
Export everything In the app: Welcome → Export everything. One zip with every course, solution, session and setting.

Does it cost anything?
The app is free. The AI is whatever you choose: a local model through Ollama or LM Studio costs nothing, and cloud providers bill you directly.

        
Do I need an account?
No. Pick a folder, pick an AI, start studying.

        
Can I use it for graded work?
Each course stores its AI policy. When a class forbids AI-written code, the coach gives hints and explanations only, never solutions to real assignments.

        
How do I move to a new computer?
Sync or copy your Library folder, install CodeCoach, and choose that folder. Or use Export everything and unzip it on the new machine.
